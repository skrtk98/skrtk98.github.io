## Digital Agency Design System (DADS)

The homepage in `homepage/` uses an original static layout inspired by the typography, link and keyboard-focus patterns in the [Digital Agency Design System](https://design.digital.go.jp/dads/). References: [Link](https://github.com/digital-go-jp/design-system-example-components/blob/main/src/components/Link/Link.tsx), [Card](https://github.com/digital-go-jp/design-system-example-components/blob/main/src/components/Card/Card.stories.tsx), and [Typography](https://github.com/digital-go-jp/design-system-example-components/blob/main/src/foundations/Typography.tsx), reviewed at commit `68b452e16d40ffaebcb195a7289696f88890cc3e`.
